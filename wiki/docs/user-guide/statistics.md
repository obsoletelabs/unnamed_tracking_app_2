# Statistics activity

The **Activity, last 52 weeks** heatmap on the Statistics Overview shows one square per day, shaded by how much you did that day: episodes you checked off plus achievements you unlocked. Open any square to see exactly what it counts.

## Using the heatmap

- **Month labels** sit above the first week of each month. On a narrow screen the grid scrolls sideways and starts at the most recent weeks.
- **Pointer:** click a square to open that day. Hovering shows the date and counts.
- **Keyboard:** Tab moves into the grid (one square is in the tab order), then the arrow keys move between days (Left and Right change the week, Up and Down change the day), Home and End jump to the first and last day. Enter or Space opens the focused day. Every square also has a spoken label such as "Saturday, 10 October 2026: 3 episodes, 1 achievement".
- **Dialog:** the same dialog opens from the keyboard and the pointer. Escape, the close button, the backdrop or **Close** dismisses it, and focus returns to the square you opened it from.

## What a day lists

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Episodes watched | Each TV show, anime or movie with the number of episodes the history log recorded that day, most first. |
| Achievements unlocked | Each achievement unlocked that day, with its game and unlock time. |
| Other history | Status changes, rewatches and ratings recorded that day. They are listed for context and are not part of the square's count. |

Titles, games and achievements link to their own detail pages. A busy day loads 50 entries at a time; choose **Show more** for the rest.

Nothing is invented. The history log records how many episodes were watched on a day, not which ones, so the dialog shows that count per title and never a made-up episode list. Achievements with no recorded unlock time, and locked achievements, appear on no day.

## Which day an entry belongs to

- Episode and other history entries are saved as a calendar day with no time of day. They stay on that day wherever you view them from.
- Achievements are saved as an exact moment. They belong to the local day of that moment in your device's time zone. The grid and the dialog both send your device's time zone, so the count on a square and the entries in its dialog always agree, including near midnight, for half-hour and 45-minute zones, and on days that are 23 or 25 hours long when clocks change.
- Moving to another time zone can move an achievement to the neighbouring day; recorded episode days do not move.

## Trash and other accounts

Only your own titles and games count. Titles and games in Trash drop out of both the squares and the dialog, and return when restored; permanently deleted ones never come back. Another account's activity never appears. Signing in with an integration or plugin credential does not give access to this page.
