# TV shows

TV shows track series metadata, seasons, episodes, watch state, ratings, lists, activity, relations, and recommendations.

Add a show manually or from configured metadata search. On the detail page, add or edit seasons, update individual episodes, or mark a season's episodes watched in bulk. Airing metadata can be refreshed for one show and by the administrator-controlled scheduled airing job.

Deleting a show moves it to trash. Restore returns the show and its season/episode data; purge permanently removes it. You can also empty the whole TV show trash or have trashed shows deleted automatically after a period you choose (never by default); see [Trash, Empty trash and automatic deletion](media-libraries.md#trash-empty-trash-and-automatic-deletion). Provider searches and refreshes require the administrator to configure the relevant credentials.

TV shows share library layouts, ranks, genre links and local pictures with movies and anime. See [Media libraries](media-libraries.md).

Add and Edit use the shared tabbed dialog described in [Media libraries](media-libraries.md#the-add-and-edit-dialogs). It never changes seasons or watched episodes of a saved show: those stay on the show's page, and a lookup's offered seasons are only created for a new show (or when you choose them in the review).
